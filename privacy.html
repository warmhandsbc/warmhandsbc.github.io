Privacy Policy

Warm Hands Home Care – Staff Clock-in App · Last updated: November 4, 2026

This policy explains how Warm Hands Home Care ("we") handles information in the Warm Hands Staff Clock-in App (the "App"). The App is only for Warm Hands staff to view their shifts and record their working time.

Information we collect

  • Google sign-in: your Google account email address and name, used only to confirm that you are a registered Warm Hands staff member.
  • Work records: your clock-in and clock-out times, the shifts assigned to you, extra-time requests and their reasons.
  • Location (optional): if you allow it, your approximate location at the moment you clock in or clock out. You can decline; clocking in still works.

How we use it

Only for scheduling, confirming attendance, payroll, client billing and service quality. We do not use it for advertising and we do not sell or share it with third parties, except where required by law.

Where it is stored

Records are stored in Warm Hands' Google Workspace account. Access is limited to authorized office staff (coordinators, administrators and finance). The App does not access your Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar or any other Google data.

How long we keep it

Work records are kept as long as needed for payroll, billing and legal record-keeping requirements, then deleted.

Your choices

You can ask to see or correct your records, or ask questions about this policy, by contacting us at info@warmhandsbc.ca or +1-778-798-8138.

私隱政策

Warm Hands Home Care 員工打卡 App · 最後更新：2026 年 11 月 4 日

本政策說明 Warm Hands Home Care（「我們」）點樣處理員工打卡 App（「App」）入面嘅資料。App 只供 Warm Hands 員工查看排更同記錄工作時間。

收集嘅資料

  • Google 登入：你 Google 帳戶嘅電郵地址同名稱，只用嚟確認你係已登記嘅 Warm Hands 員工。
  • 工作紀錄：開工 / 收工打卡時間、你嘅排更、額外時間申請同原因。
  • 位置（可選）：如果你允許，記錄打卡嗰一刻嘅大概位置。你可以唔允許，照樣可以打卡。

用途

只用於排更、確認出勤、計算人工、客人收費同服務質素。唔會用作廣告，亦唔會出售或者提供俾第三方（法律要求除外）。

儲存

紀錄儲存喺 Warm Hands 嘅 Google Workspace 帳戶，只有獲授權嘅辦公室職員（Coordinator、管理員、Finance）可以存取。App 唔會讀取你嘅 Gmail、Google 雲端硬碟、日曆或者其他 Google 資料。

保存期

工作紀錄會按人工、收費同法律要求保存所需時間，之後刪除。

查詢

想查閱或更正你嘅紀錄，或者對本政策有疑問，請聯絡 info@warmhandsbc.ca 或 +1-778-798-8138。
